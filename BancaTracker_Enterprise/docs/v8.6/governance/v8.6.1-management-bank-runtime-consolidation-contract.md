# BancaTracker Enterprise v8.6.1

## Management Bank Runtime Consolidation Contract

**Status:** Approved corrective-release implementation contract. This contract was defined before implementation; v8.6.1 now implements it through `BancaTrackerManagementBank` and the isolated Management Scorecard integration. This document remains authoritative for Management Bank identity, consolidation, Budget ownership, and compatibility.

**Foundation:** This contract specializes the future hierarchy described by [Step 6C.2](./step6c2-bank-identity-budget-ownership-contract.md). It does not replace or weaken that contract.

## 1. Problem and consumer boundary

The current Management Scorecard and global Bank filter use the normalized PR `fact.bank` identity directly. Distinct governed source identities such as `INDIAN BANK` and `INDIAN BANK (PMSBY)` therefore appear as peer Banks and an `INDIAN BANK` selection excludes its PMSBY child.

The v8.6.1 migration boundary is limited to:

- Management Scorecard Bank rows, contribution, ranking, priority, and derived management cue/narrative;
- Management Bank filtering;
- authoritative parent Target lookup required by those Management consumers; and
- top-level Performance MIS Bank presentation only if implementation evidence proves it is the same Management-level consumer.

## 2. Additive identity hierarchy

`sourceBank` is the existing normalized operational/source identity held in `fact.bank`. It remains unchanged and distinguishable.

`subChannel` is governed metadata. v8.6.1 supports `CORE` and `PMSBY`. It must come from an exact explicit mapping or the controlled configured-Bank compatibility rule. It must never be inferred by parsing parentheses, stripping text, substring or regular-expression matching, fuzzy matching, or `BANK_ALIASES`.

`managementBank` is the governed analytical parent. It is additive metadata and must not replace or mutate `fact.bank` or `canonicalBank`.

Resolution provenance is one of:

- `GOVERNED`: an exact source mapping exists;
- `LEGACY_SELF_MAPPED`: an otherwise unmapped source is an exact member of the existing configured Bank universe and is treated as `managementBank = sourceBank`, `subChannel = CORE`;
- `UNMAPPED`: no exact governed mapping or permitted configured self-map exists; or
- `INVALID`: the source identity is absent or not a non-empty string.

The static representation has one entry per exact source identity, so it cannot express competing mappings. `CONFLICT` is therefore not a v8.6.1 status.

Repository configuration supports `ODISHA GRAMEEN BANK` and `TAMIL NADU GRAMA BANK`. They remain `UNMAPPED` until business governance confirms their exact normalized source identities and intended parents. No fuzzy repair is permitted.

No additional scheme/sub-channel source identities were found in the v8.6.0 repository.

## 4. Actual and reconciliation authority

Every accepted atomic fact contributes its signed premium to at most one Management Bank. Parent Actual is derived, never independently added:

```text
Management Actual = sum(atomic source Actual mapped to the Management Bank)
Atomic All-Bank Actual = mapped Management Actual + explicit unmapped Actual
```

For `INDIAN BANK` CORE Actual 80 and PMSBY Actual 20, Management Actual is 100. A stored or independently computed parent aggregate must not be added to those facts.

Unmapped facts retain source identity and signed Actual, remain visible in reconciliation, and remain in the scoped atomic denominator.

## 5. Budget and achievement authority

The approved v8.6.1 rule is:

```text
Management Budget = authoritative Target stored against the parent Management Bank
Management Achievement = consolidated Management Actual / Management Budget
```

A separately represented PMSBY flat Target is legacy/atomic context only and is not additive. If `INDIAN BANK` Target is 120 and `INDIAN BANK (PMSBY)` Target is 25, Management Budget remains 120, not 145. Percentages are recalculated from consolidated amounts; child percentages are never summed.

Commercial Budget and Potential retain their existing `branchId + periodKey` ownership and reconciliation. This contract does not redesign them or Target seasonality. The Management consumer resolves the annual Target for the exact parent Bank and then uses the existing parent Bank seasonality behavior.

## 6. Contribution and filtering

```text
Management Contribution % = Management Bank Actual / scoped atomic All-Bank Actual * 100
```

For atomic Actuals 80 Indian CORE, 20 Indian PMSBY, 50 IOB CORE, 10 IOB PMSBY, and 40 Other, the denominator is 200 and Management contributions are 50%, 30%, and 20%. Unrounded contributions reconcile to 100% when the denominator is positive; display rounding is the only permitted presentation difference.

Selecting Management Bank `INDIAN BANK` includes every fact whose exact governed resolution has `managementBank = INDIAN BANK`, including CORE and PMSBY, and excludes unrelated or unmapped facts. Returned facts retain their original `fact.bank` and all atomic attribution.

## 7. Child identity and compatibility

Consolidation must preserve source Bank, sub-channel, branch/`branchId`, branch code, IMD, BA code, RM/assigned RM, LOB, business type, product code/name, and existing booking attribution. None may be reconstructed from the Management Bank display label.

Existing `fact.bank` and `canonicalBank` behavior remains available. Management Bank is not a global replacement. Existing RM Productivity, Branch Activation, branch-universe denominators, opportunity ownership, Reference Priority, commercial branch-period reconciliation, maturity/movement, and commercial driver semantics remain unchanged.

## 8. Explicit non-goals

- fuzzy or alias-based parent inference;
- raw `INTERMEDIARY` ingestion redesign;
- physical-branch deduplication across source identities;
- commercial Budget/Potential redesign;
- wholesale `canonicalBank` migration;
- Master Data Administration UI or new persistence architecture; and
- changes to existing v8.6.0 contracts.

## 9. Acceptance criteria

The executable contract in `tests/step-v861-management-bank-runtime-consolidation-contract.test.js` specifies exact mapping, fallback, Actual, Budget, contribution, filtering, attribution preservation, canonical compatibility, Target seasonality compatibility, and operational-consumer isolation. It is a permanent regression test for the implemented runtime authority.
